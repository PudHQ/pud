## Hot Metal

The generated press bed was reoriented as a mirror-readable forme and encoded as an 860-pixel WebP. The print separates the raised PUD letters and mascot engraving from that same image, producing a correctly reading impression. The roller, source sheet and saved sidecar are implemented separately.

```text
A beautifully art-directed, tightly cropped MACRO PHOTOGRAPH of the bed of a real vintage letterpress loaded with enormous metal and ebony wood printing type, portrait-ish square composition, viewed straight down with just a hint of the raised metal edges, lit rakingly from upper left. Main subject: three very large unmistakable chunky block-faced letterpress type slugs spelling P U D in reverse as a true physical print forme, with a tiny round period sort beside them; top half is almost all these huge chunky, deeply extruded brass and worn lead letterforms seen from above, staggered on locked-up rectangular slugs. Aged wooden furniture, cast iron quoins, metal spacers and small metal braces locking the types on a heavy chase. Lower third has a rectangular engraved metal ornament printing block showing a vintage custard flan pudding mascot with round spectacles and a tiny bow tie and faint ornamental hatch texture. Tight crop the type and blocks fill whole edge-to-edge frame asymmetrically. Gorgeously tactile black ink deposits, bright silver edges, used copper and dull lead, occasional historic red enameled surfaces, mesmerizing deep shadows. Restrained dark palette neutral black steel, ink-black, tarnished silver, rim of warm brass. Editorial still life photographed in an actual letterpress studio, no bokeh, no graphic overlay, no labels or miscellaneous words or numbers, no illustrated faux-3D or plastic CGI look. Material authenticity and crisp beautiful shapes. Editorial hero image for an experimental print shop intertwined with a developer tool.
```
