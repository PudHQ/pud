## Crash Test

```text

A chunky tiny scarlet vintage crash-test coupe carrying a cheerful caramel flan pudding engineer with round dark spectacles and a little red bow tie. Left-to-right it collides nose first into an immense matte butter-yellow rubber numeral 0 bolted upright on a dark factory test sled. Warm sunset orange backdrop and meticulous brushed steel, extremely punchy studio light, practical miniature models photographed on real film, sharp paint and rubber textures, witty and dangerous-looking without injury, side-on strong graphic silhouettes and great craft. The red car and shaped ZERO are the entire story. No typography, watermarks, captions, NO other numerals or symbols.
```

## Flipbook

**Web files** — `flip-0.webp`, `flip-1.webp`, `flip-2.webp`, `flip-3.webp`. The panels were cut from one sheet in reading order, from top left to bottom right. Each frame is 768 × 512.

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Design one original 2-by-2 model sheet of exactly four sequential individual frames from a 1940s animation flipbook for a TypeScript proof tool called Pud. A charming squat custard pudding wearing shiny caramel cap, round thick black spectacles, red bow tie, and simple gloved noodle arms. The four equal rectangular panels have entirely blank plain warm ivory backgrounds, no borders, absolutely no words/letters/numbers. In each of the four frames the SAME lovable small pudding is seen from the same side-on view increasingly pushing a large triangular stack of domino-size wooden blocks to the right: first leaning backward and studying the obstacle, second both hands planted leaning hard, third in a dynamic shoving pose the blocks sliding, fourth triumphant arms raised at rest beside the moved triangular stack. One Pud and one triangular stack in each frame, generous blank padding at panel boundaries so they can be cut precisely at 50% width and 50% height. Character and pile should occupy roughly 75% of each cell. Inspired by early hand-inked animation drawings, expressive black india ink silhouettes, sparse duplicate motion contour pencil, vermilion spot accents, warm butter custard color. Coherent, graphic, old animation studio exposure sheet, ink and paper, superb exuberant tiny character acting. Landscape format, 3:2 full sheet, each panel about 3:2. Background is flat, each frame fully separate. No panels within panels, no modern 3D, no watermarks.
```

### Original prompt

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. A precision high-speed photograph contact sheet of a caramel custard pudding wobbling on a small, matte ivory dinner plate. Four equally sized photographic frames in a perfectly straight 2 by 2 square grid with hard square edges, no gaps, gutters, frame art, captions or typography. Each quarter continuous identical deep brick red seamless background and consistent warm side lighting, locked photostudio camera at plate level. The gorgeous glossy golden flan (no face) and plate are entirely visible in each frame, with generous headroom and soft shadows. First frame top left: an upright still flan. Top right: flan leans visibly left with inertia. Bottom left: flan leans dramatically right. Bottom right: flan settles squat and wide. Looks like 1970s Kodak Ektachrome, tactile subtle grain, dramatic copper highlights, analog cookbook photography. The grid will be sliced into four separate photographs used for a motion timeline. No text, letters, numerals, guides, borders or dividers.
```
