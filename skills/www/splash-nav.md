# Splash navigation fieldnotes

On a one-screen splash, a short project note can open directly from the About tab.
Clip the tab’s background and keep its rectangular touch target at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. [WCAG enhanced target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-enhanced.html)
At phone width, measure the gap under the open tab, then press Escape and check that keyboard focus returns to it.
A signup link should focus the field on every activation, with a visible focus ring even when reduced motion turns off its animation.
