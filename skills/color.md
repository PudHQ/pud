# Color fieldnotes

Compare colors against their actual neighbors and at the size they will occupy on the page. [Albers, *Interaction of Color*](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300179354/interaction-of-color/)
For a complementary pair, try hues roughly 180° apart in OKLCH. [CSS Color 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#ok-lab)
On Pud, vermilion (`#ff462d`) fills a broad field and cyan (`#00bbd5`) picks out narrow edges, 179° apart in hue.
Pud’s ink (`#17171c`) and paper (`#f4f0e6`) have a relative-luminance contrast of 15.7:1, above the WCAG AA minimums of 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text. [WCAG contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)
