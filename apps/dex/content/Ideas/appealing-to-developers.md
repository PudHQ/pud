# Appealing to developers

The success of pud Pud is going to depend -- principally -- on how well I can communicate to developers that Pud is:

1. An interesting and novel tool (intrigue)
2. Something that helps them feel like they're getting ahead by using it(insider advantage)
3. A way to learn something new (educationally beneficial)

While I do think that the overarching way to win most developers over is to make them feel like they're part of a special club, or that they're cool, or that they've got the inside scoop on a particular technology, you still have to build something that genuinely works, and works really well.
