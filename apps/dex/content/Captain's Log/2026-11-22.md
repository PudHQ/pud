## The ideal interface

So. What does the ideal interface look like? I was hoping that we could try to farm some answers with `Astra 6.2 Ultra 🌠` yesterday -- which produced this ["Alpha Design Experiment" doc.](https://github.com/rorz/pud/tree/main/experiments/alpha-design) I was _trying_ in earnest to get it to write out some ideas for product direction, features, and interfaces for what formal verification could look like for your everyday software engineer. Considering the amount of tokens I burned creating that cache in particular I'm quite surprised how hard it is to read and follow.[^1]

In other words:

![[skeleton-wait.png]]

Not wanting to sound like a broken record here, but getting the interface for Pud right will be the make or break of it. What do I mean by "interface"? Well, I mean it generally in the software-tooling sense... How do _you_ as a _dev_ interact with -- and use -- Pud? What, specifically, do you have to:

- Set up or "install"
- Tell your yourself or your agent to "configure"
  - Is this a file? A collection of files?
- Have to write (or get your agent to write) in order to:
  i. Integrate this into existing code
  ii. Write new code
- How do you maintain this?

Can we come up with something that sparks joy and intrigue? Can we make something like [`Drizzle`](https://orm.drizzle.team/) which took the \[dev\] world by storm because of its TypeScript-ification of Postgres modelling and operations-building?
