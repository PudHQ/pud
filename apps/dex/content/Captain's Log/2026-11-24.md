## Lean-In?

Lean seems to be capturing the mindshare of the community right now. Not least because apparently the Riemann Hypothesis [has been solved](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math/) using Lean. I think unfortunately for the other proof assistant / languages -- i.e. Rocq and Dafny -- this will mean it's going to take them longer to "catch up" and be cool.

So, I've set out making some [[measuring-typescript-to-lean-coverage|Lean examples]] today. I'm also trying to model something closer to Drizzle so that we're not married to the idea of having to stick to one proof assistant.
