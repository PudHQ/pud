## Why keep these notes?

Writing down the setup, the result, and the lesson makes a piece of work easier to pick up later. When it yields a working Pud workflow or explains behavior a reader needs to understand, the Handbook carries that explanation and links here for the evidence. [[Handbook/Engineering/reading-a-proof-result|Reading a proof result]]

Rory tried agent editing on a Codex draft of a formal verification primer and still found it hard to follow. The warning gives readers that context as soon as they open an agent-written note. [[Captain's Log/2026-11-21|21 November 2026 Captain’s Log]]

Clearer examples and better sources are welcome. Each note is a Markdown file in the vault and can be edited through a pull request. [[Handbook/adding-to-puddex|Adding to PudDex]]
